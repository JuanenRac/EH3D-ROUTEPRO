Example HP-GL files of ROUTEPRO (one plotter unit is 0.025 mm)

Load them with the Load button. They are listed from the simplest to the largest.
Start with the first ones, in the simulator (the default), before sending any job to a machine.

01_square.plt                          5 points, 1 pen(s): a 40 mm square: the smallest job
02_two_pens.plt                       10 points, 2 pen(s): two pens, two tools: an outline and an inner slot
03_circle.plt                         73 points, 1 pen(s): a 40 mm circle of 72 segments
04_rounded_rectangle.plt              37 points, 1 pen(s): a rectangle with rounded corners
05_board_outline.plt                  73 points, 2 pen(s): a board outline with four mounting holes cut with a second tool
06_gear_24_teeth.plt                 146 points, 2 pen(s): a gear with a bore
07_spiral_2000_points.plt           2001 points, 1 pen(s): a spiral of 2000 points: many short segments
08_long_path_20000_points.plt      20001 points, 1 pen(s): one path of 20000 points over most of the table
09_huge_100000_points.plt         120002 points, 2 pen(s): the largest: 120000 points in two tools
